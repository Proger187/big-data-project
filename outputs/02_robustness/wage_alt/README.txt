Aiym: wage analysis (Y3)

Outcome: y3_log_wage.
Training years: 2016 and 2019.
Within-year split: 70% training, 30% held-out testing.
Model selection: five-fold cross-validation within training data.
Model fitting and prediction-error metrics are unweighted.
Descriptive means and cross-year mean gaps use PWGTP weights.
Selected models: age spline with df = 5, excluding race.
Race comparisons are included as sensitivity checks.
Cross-year forecasts: 2016 to 2019 and 2019 to 2022.
Adjusted gap sign: adjusted predicted value minus actual value.
The adjusted gap is not an identified causal effect of COVID.

Status: overall wage analysis completed.
Working-hours analysis (Y4) has not yet been completed.
Raw data and person-level predictions are not included.
